About KrishiGPT

An agriculture-focused AI assistant designed to make farming information easier to access and understand.

KrishiGPT is an AI-powered agriculture assistant that helps users ask questions about crops, planting, soil, irrigation, weather and sustainable farming. It combines an open-source AI model with weather information to make responses more relevant to the user's location.
